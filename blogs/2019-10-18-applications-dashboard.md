The Chef Automate Applications dashboard allows you to organize and display your applications data from Chef Habitat in an intuitive way. Chef Automate organizes data from the application and environment tags provided by the Chef Habitat supervisor. Under the Chef Automate Applications dashboard, the Service Groups display lets you understand the status of your Chef Habitat services, even when scaling out to large numbers of services.
