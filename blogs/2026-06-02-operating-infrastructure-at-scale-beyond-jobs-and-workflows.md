Organizations are shifting away from treating production infrastructure as isolated servers, as modern shared environments like Kubernetes clusters and large pooled estates require management at greater scale with higher expectations for visibility and predictability. A modern operating model must link execution with ongoing validation, enabling teams to detect drift early, maintain compliance, and act with confidence. The post presents Chef 360 as a solution for combining automation with operational visibility across diverse environments.
