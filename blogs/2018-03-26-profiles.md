Overview Compliance profiles help you secure your infrastructure continuously. Chef Automate compliance profiles translate CIS Benchmarks and other security standards into easily readable policy. You can install and download one of our 300+ ready-to-use compliance profiles from Profiles, or upload your custom profiles.
